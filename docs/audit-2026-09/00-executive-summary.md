## 1. Executive Summary

**Verdict: a genuinely differentiated product on an unusually disciplined codebase, sitting on an unlaunchable operational and security foundation. Do not launch publicly in the current state.**

Hisaab has real, defensible product ground. The competitive research (report 11) confirms nobody else combines a two-sided consent-based udhaar ledger, a tracker-only kameti with a provably-fair draw and public witness link, roman-Urdu-first framing, free conversational AI entry, and an anti-Splitwise pricing story — an intersection no funded competitor occupies. The code itself is ahead of its stage: enforced layering with one DAL, effectively zero `any` in 69k lines of strict TypeScript, ~90 test files over the pure money math, a documented compensation pattern, a best-in-class local reminder engine, an exemplary motion/reduced-motion system, and a self-documentation culture (`tasks/lessons.md`, `ARCH-RECON`) most seed-stage teams fake after the fact (reports 02, 08, 09, 13).

Three things make it unlaunchable today:

1. **The production security state is unprovable.** The entire trust boundary — RLS policies and SECURITY DEFINER RPCs for a multi-tenant money app — lives in **40 hand-applied `supabase-migration-*.sql` files** (46 `supabase-*.sql` total, verified this pass) with no runner, no applied-state ledger, no drift detection, functions redefined in up to five files, a live alphabetical-order apply trap, and the repo's own tracker showing the production security-verification SQL was never run (05 C1/M1, 04 F-MIG1, 02 C-1, 13). Under the *baseline* schema, any authenticated user can self-insert into any group as a 'connected' member; the fix exists only in an unverified migration, and the shipped client already calls an RPC that a later migration drops. Every "fixed" security claim in this audit is conditional on Studio state nobody can currently prove.
2. **Confirmed cross-user money-integrity defects.** Account deletion hard-deletes a user's expenses inside shared groups — and an owner's deletion destroys the whole group's ledger for everyone (03 C1, CONFIRMED by both adversarial verifiers). Ex-members can rewrite or hard-delete historical group expenses and settlements, silently shifting everyone's balances (05 H4, 04 F-RLS1). Loans, group expenses, and settlements have no optimistic locking, so two-device edits lose real money records (12 F-2/F-6/F-7). Multi-leg money moves can commit halfway server-side on flaky networks with no repair queue — the offline outbox is an inert scaffold whose handlers all throw (07 MF-01, 12 F-4).
3. **The trust-positioned marketing is falsifiable in week one.** The Play listing claims a PIN lock (dead code — `PinLockScreen.tsx` has zero importers), offline-first entry (all mutations hard-fail offline), and USD/EUR/GBP (unsupported). For a product whose entire pitch is "nothing to leak, no bank passwords," discoverable false security claims are the fastest possible way to lose the market and invite Play enforcement (11 critical finding, 12 F-1, 06 UX-26, 01).

Two premise corrections this summary carries against earlier reports: the app's **code default language is English, not Urdu** (`src/lib/i18n.ts:3062` — `|| "en"`), so every "Urdu-first" impact claim in reports 07/08/09 inherits doc/code drift (06 UX-04 is correct); and a **strict meta-CSP does exist** at `index.html:22` — report 13's "no CSP" claim is wrong; the real gap is HTTP-header delivery (frame-ancestors, HSTS, reporting — 05 M7). Report 11's "no global search" claim (G8) is also wrong — `src/components/GlobalSearch.tsx` exists. The `delete_current_user` FK-abort finding was **REFUTED** by live Postgres reproduction (03, 04) and is excluded here; a belt-and-braces ordered delete remains cheap hygiene.

**Bottom line for the founder:** the wedge is real and the craft is real, but the audit found 1 systemic critical, 2 confirmed money-corruption criticals, 1 critical false-claims cluster, and ~20 confirmed highs. Most fixes are S/M effort — the pre-launch critical list is roughly 3–5 focused weeks of work plus one non-negotiable action that takes an afternoon: **run the verification SQL against production and commit the output.** Until that happens, no other security statement in this audit — good or bad — is meaningful.
